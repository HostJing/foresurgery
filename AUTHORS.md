# Authors

All authors are at Dalian University of Technology, affiliated with both the
Key Laboratory of Intelligent Control and Optimization for Industrial Equipment
of Ministry of Education and the School of Control Science and Engineering.

| name | role in this repository |
|---|---|
| Jingjie Li | experiment design, dispatch model, surgery operator, analysis, maintainer |
| Xutong Zhang | data pipeline, warehouse construction, clustering |
| Yintang Li | attribution arithmetic, bootstrap intervals, figure generation |
| Haoyu Wu | control experiments (replanning, sweeps, synthetic errors) |
| Xue Zhang | supervision, problem formulation, review |

Corresponding author: Xue Zhang.

The repository accompanies the manuscript *Forecast value concentrates
day-ahead: counterfactual surgery on issued rolling forecasts from 70
industrial microgrids*. Citation metadata is in `CITATION.cff`; the code is
maintained by Jingjie Li, who is the right person to contact about the
pipeline (see `README.md`).
